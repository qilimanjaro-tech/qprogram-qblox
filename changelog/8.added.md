`qp.qblox.scope(bus, *, averaging=True, trigger_level=None, name=None)` records the raw ADC trace of the QRM that drives a readout bus, for the whole run, and returns a `MeasurementHandle` to pass to `result.get(scope, field="raw")`. Scopes are named `scope0`, `scope1`, ... on a counter of their own, so adding one never renames an acquisition.
