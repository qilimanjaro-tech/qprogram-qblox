Validating a program that plays an `IQDrag` against `qblox-default-v1` no longer raises `AttributeError: 'IQDrag' object has no attribute 'sigma'`. qprogram 1.0 gives `IQDrag` the parameters of qililab's DRAG, `num_sigmas` and `drag_coefficient` in place of `sigma` and `beta`, and the profile's check read `sigma` on every DRAG play, swept or not.
